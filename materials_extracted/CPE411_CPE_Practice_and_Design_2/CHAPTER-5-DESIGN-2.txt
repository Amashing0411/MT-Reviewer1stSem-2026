Summary of Findings, Conclusions, and Recommendations 
7. Summary of Findings 
 Definition: A concise recap of results obtained from tests, feasibility studies, and 
analyses. 
 Example: “The irrigation system achieved a 30% water savings, was technically stable, 
and had high user acceptance among farmers.”  
 Use in Research: Summarizes evidence to support conclusions.  


--- PAGE 2 ---
 
8. Conclusions 
 Definition: Final interpretation of research results, showing whether objectives were 
achieved. 
 Example: “The smart irrigation system is technically and economically feasible, and it 
can significantly improve farming efficiency.”  
 Use in Research: Demonstrates the project’s overall success and relevance.

9. Recommendations 
 Definition: Suggestions for improvement, future research, or wider application of the 
project. 
 Example: “Future work should integrate solar panels for energy independence and 
expand the system for large-scale farming.” 
 Use in Research: Guides stakeholders, researchers, or future developers on how to bui ld 
upon the project.
